# Rosario’s Cafe — research notes
Researched: 2026-09-07 (flagship rebuild). Demo only.

## Facts
- Address: 18 Northumberland Place, Bath BA1 5AR
- Phone: 01225 469590 · email rosariosbath@gmail.com
- Official site: https://www.rosarioscafe.co.uk/
- Run by Rosario Bavetta — Italian cafe in Grade II Georgian building; outdoor tables + upstairs seating
- Coffee/tea: D J Miles, Porlock, Somerset
- Signature: Sicilian cannoli & pastries; homemade cakes; strong gluten-free range
- Two shopfronts nearly opposite each other on Northumberland Place (guest notes)
- Google ~4.6 · TripAdvisor ~4.5 (via Sluurpy aggregators)

## Hours (official contact / menus pages — confirm before visiting)
- Mon–Wed & Sat: 8am–5pm (kitchen to 4pm)
- Thu–Fri: 8am–7pm (kitchen to 6pm)
- Sun: 9:30am–4pm
- Aggregators sometimes disagree (e.g. Thu closed / Sun 10–3) — prefer official site
