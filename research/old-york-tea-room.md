# The Old York Tea Room — research notes
Researched: 2026-09-07 (flagship rebuild). Demo only.

## Facts
- Address: 72 Goodramgate, York YO1 7LF (Lady Row / Our Lady’s Row)
- Phone listings: 01904 624247
- No dedicated official website; Google ~4.9
- Hours aggregators: Wed–Sat ~9–4, Sun ~9:30–3, Mon–Tue closed
- Owners: Tony & Thomas Vickers (Thomas also referred to as Thomas Bojaczuk in 2023 Press)
- ~14 covers upstairs; Grade I Lady Row 1316/17; stairs, no lift
- Menu cues: homemade cakes, cream tea, cinnamon bun, Turkish coffee, Afternoon Experience for Two (£43.99 — House of York Substack, Feb 2025)
- YorkMix Choice Awards 2024 Gold
- Second unit: Old York Cake Shop, 38 High Petergate (announced)
